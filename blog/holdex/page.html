About

A Project to redesign a Web3 website with the goal of making it more approachable and user-friendly. Focused on clarity, trust, and conversion, ensuring blockchain content feels accessible to a wider audience.

Task

Create a redesign of the current website to have a better design and converts.

Year

May 1, 2025

Services

UI/UX Designer

Category

WebDesign

Challenges

Help holder visitors clearly understand about the services being provided.

The website currently presents large blocks of dense text, which can overwhelm users and create cognitive overload, making it harder for them to quickly grasp key information. In addition, essential actions such as accessing the “Services” section or button are not immediately visible, reducing overall usability and task efficiency. The lack of interactive elements throughout the website also makes it feel static and less engaging, which weakens the overall user experience. Finally, the calls-to-action (CTAs) are not visually distinct or strategically emphasized, limiting their effectiveness in guiding users toward desired actions.

Proposed Solutions?

To improve usability, an easily visible “Services” tab was added to the navigation bar, along with a preview section on the homepage to provide quick access. A dedicated “Services” page was also created to present clearer and more structured information for users. To make the website feel more engaging, interactive elements were introduced, adding a sense of dynamism and improving overall user experience. Finally, calls-to-action (CTAs) were strengthened and visually emphasized to better capture attention and effectively guide users toward the intended actions.
